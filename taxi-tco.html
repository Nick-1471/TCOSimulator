Why utilisation and financing are the sliders that matter most

A taxi is a revenue asset, not a household expense, so the two variables that actually decide this comparison are different from a private car:

  • Utilisation multiplies everything. A vehicle doing 90,000 km a year recovers a large capital premium roughly three times faster than one doing 30,000 km a year, because fixed costs are spread over far more revenue-generating kilometres.
  • Financing cost is often as large a lever as the vehicle price. Informal-sector rates of 24% or more, over a short term, can add more to the total cost than a change in the purchase price itself.
  • Charging location changes the revenue picture, not just the running cost. Depot charging overnight costs nothing in lost revenue. Relying on public fast charging during the day removes the vehicle from service and shortens battery life.

How the model is built

  1. Capital. Purchase price minus resale value at the end of the horizon, using a type-specific resale curve. Where the effective EV lifespan is reached inside the horizon, a second vehicle purchase (net of a small salvage value) is added in that year.
  2. Financing interest. A standard amortising loan at the chosen rate and term. Interest is calculated month by month and kept separate from capital depreciation, so the two can be read apart in the breakdown table.
  3. Energy. Diesel or electricity for the year's kilometres, at the escalated price for that year. Diesel and electricity have historically escalated at very different rates in South Africa, which is why both are separate, adjustable sliders.
  4. Maintenance, insurance and tyres. Annual amounts rising with general cost inflation. Tyres are replaced on a distance-based interval.
  5. Charging downtime. Where charging relies on public fast charging during the operating day, the model adds a cost for the revenue the vehicle would otherwise have earned during that time. Depot or overnight charging carries no such cost.
  6. Effective EV lifespan. The base lifespan slider is reduced automatically where fast charging and climate extremes are both selected, since these accelerate battery degradation.
