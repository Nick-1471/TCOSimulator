Thinking about a second, urban-only car — for a commute or local errands, with another vehicle already covering longer trips? This compares three paths: keep what you have, replace it with a smaller petrol runabout, or replace it with a solar-assisted EV. Every number below is a starting estimate — drag the sliders to match your own driving, prices and local tariffs.

How to read this: figures are today's Rand terms, escalated by the rates you set — this is a planning estimate, not a quote or financial advice, and none of the underlying prices (especially the EV's landed cost) are confirmed by any manufacturer or distributor. Battery, depreciation and solar-coverage assumptions are informed guesses you should adjust to match whatever real quotes you're able to get.

Why the lines dip below zero early on: a car depreciates fastest in its first couple of years. If you sold right then, that drop in resale value can outweigh what you've spent driving it — a negative number just means that option would leave you financially ahead of having spent nothing at all. It's temporary; every option turns positive once a few years of running costs build up.
